title: 3D Business Card Visualizer
project_url: https://github.com/KonradJam/business-card-viewer
categories:
  - dtp
  - web
  - graphic
technologies:
  - JavaScript
  - Three.js
  - PDF.js
problem: Clients often find it difficult to understand how finishes such as spot
  UV varnish or embossing will look on the final business card, because in
  production files they are usually shown as black-and-white masks.
solution: This web application renders PDF files and displays the business card
  as a 3D object, presenting those finishing effects in a more realistic way
  using Three.js and PDF.js.
impact: It makes it easier to show clients the final result before printing,
  reduces misunderstandings, and helps evaluate the design more quickly.
